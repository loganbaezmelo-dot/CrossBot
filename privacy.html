Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

The CrossNetwork family of bots (including CrossBot℠ and CrossMod℠) is committed to protecting user privacy. This policy outlines how data is handled across all our services:

  • Data Collection: Cross Network bots do not collect, log, store, or transmit any user data, messages, server histories, or personal information to external databases or third-party servers.
  • Real-Time Message Processing: Messages and command interactions are processed entirely in real-time within volatile memory via Bot Designer for Discord to process triggers or issue moderation actions, and are immediately discarded.
  • Moderation Actions: Information supplied during moderation actions (such as user mentions or warning reasons) is processed only for sending direct notifications to users and channel embeds, and is not permanently stored by the bots.
  • Third-Party Sharing: Because no data is collected or stored, no data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
