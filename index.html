🤖 CrossBot℠

CrossBot℠ is an interactive chat bot built using carefully curated responses instead of live AI. It scans your text chat for specific phrases and instantly fires back custom replies.

Equipped with built-in triggers, it tracks gaming updates, shares trivia, and keeps server chats active! Use /help or your master prefix to see its capabilities.

🛠️ CrossMod℠

CrossMod℠ is a streamlined, leak-proof moderation bot built to keep your server safe and organized with precise slash commands.

Equipped with essential features like /warn, /kick, and /ban, it sends DM notifications directly to users while keeping action logs clean and private.

🎟️ CrossTicket℠

CrossTicket℠ is a lightweight and secure support ticket bot designed for seamless member assistance.

Deploy interactive panel buttons with /ticket-panel and organize support channels with /set-category. Creates private, staff-accessible channels automatically!
